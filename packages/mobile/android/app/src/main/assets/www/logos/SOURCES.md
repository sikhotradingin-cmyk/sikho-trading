Company marks are bundled locally for issuer identification in the simulated-trading catalogue. They are authentic assets retrieved from the issuers' public websites or official newsroom/annual-report pages on 16 November 2026. No endorsement is implied. Trademark/copyright ownership remains with each issuer; no general redistribution licence is claimed. Review distribution rights if these assets are used outside this identification context.

`sources.json` records the exact official source pages, asset URLs, current catalogue ISINs, retrieval dates, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes for 17 issuers. Artwork is unmodified and displayed with contain sizing. SBI uses its official website favicon because its published header-logo URL returned 404. Aadhar, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, ITC and Bajaj Finance have sourced artwork, alongside 11 additional prominent issuers. Unsupported issuers use labelled monogram fallbacks, not invented official logos. Existing benchmark/commodity drawings are application pictograms.

ISIN is preferred to display aliases. Stock futures/options resolve the exact underlying issuer. Symbol prefixes such as HDFC/BAJFIN are not used to identify unrelated issuers. Old Bajaj Finance ISIN INE296A01024 is retained as an explicit historical identity alongside current INE296A01032. NSE/BSE listings with the same ISIN share the asset.
